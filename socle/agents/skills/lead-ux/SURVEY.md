# The survey and the feedback — formats

Read at steps 1, 2 and 5 of [SKILL.md](./SKILL.md).

## `survey.md`

These sections, in this order. Each applies only when it has something to
say.

- **What the real data says** — one row per finding, with the count or the
  example:

  | What the data shows | What it changes |
  |---|---|

- **The screen as it is** — only when the screen exists:
  - its content block by block:

    | Block | What it shows | Its source | The decision it serves |
    |---|---|---|---|

  - four lists: doubles, what serves nothing, missing actions, and the real
    journey step by step;
  - the usage problems, ranked blocking, major, minor.

- **Asked for or placed** — each row carries the quote or the path:

  | What | Where the human asked for or placed it |
  |---|---|

  It is re-read for each direction before anything is removed, lightened or
  moved.

- **To keep** — the answer to *what must not move*, quoted from
  `round-0/feedback.md`; in one shot, the agent's reading of the audit,
  marked as such.

- **What other products do** — each row cites the research file:

  | Product | What we take | Why |
  |---|---|---|

- A last line per round: `Completed after round <n>: …`.

## `round-<n>/feedback.md`

- A heading `# Round <n> — feedback`, the date, and the absolute path of the
  page shown.
- One section per direction and one for the whole page, each a table, the
  words in quotes, unedited:

  | The human's words | The reading |
  |---|---|

- For round 0, a section *What must not move*, in the same form.
- *What the next round changes*: a list, each item citing the words it
  comes from.
- *Corrections of the reading*: filled when the human corrects one.
