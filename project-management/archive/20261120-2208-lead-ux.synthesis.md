# Synthesis — Lead UX: usage before technique, and a push only after the local gates

## What changed and why

On 2026-11-20 a page coded straight from a spec, with no research, was
judged « hyper naïf comme regroupement », « pas assez dense ». The research
that followed on the Olympe project, three short rounds in under four
hours, was judged « very top ». Its report names what made the difference:
the human's words kept as said, real data from the first round, a survey
written before drawing, directions that really differ shown side by side,
short rounds, the feedback written down verbatim between rounds, and an eye
that is never the builder's own. This pull request puts that method into
the socle as the `lead-ux` skill. Chisel must work without the Harness, so
the skill carries its own means: a capture script, a directions page
template, the researcher's brief, the eye's brief and the formats of the
survey and the feedback.

The workflow now says it. In step 2 ("Write or sharpen the spec"), when a
task touches what a person sees or does, the analysis starts from usage,
before the technique. The Architect names the size, and the human can
overrule it: a new screen, flow or redesign runs the full rounds before the
spec leaves `draft`; a small request on an existing screen runs one quick
round; a light change writes its journey in one line. Each round is a stop
for the human, listed in "Where the work stops". A brief for an analysis
states the usage first, and the discipline table routes to the skill.

The second request is small: after the close, the Architect pushes only
once the gate commands pass locally. CI confirms, it does not discover.
Each change ships its intention, `lead-ux-before-screens` and
`push-after-local-gates`, so updated repos learn of it.

## Critical decisions

- **Three deliverables, files beside the spec, and those files are the
  memory.** `ux/survey.md`, `ux/round-<n>/directions.html` and
  `ux/round-<n>/feedback.md` live in the task workspace. A round restarts
  from them, never from a session. The alternative, a research held in a
  conversation, is what lost the context on 21-11.
- **Size first, said in one line.** Light change, quick round, full
  rounds. Running the full method on a label change was the cost to avoid.
  A single rule ("always research") was left aside: it would make the skill
  a tax and get it skipped.
- **One preferred direction, always.** The template refuses a page without
  exactly one, through a console error the report shows. In one shot the
  preferred direction counts as chosen, and the page stays for the human to
  read afterwards. Round 0 is skipped there (Architect's ruling 4).
- **The eye is bounded and never the builder.** One fresh *mid* sub-agent
  per round, over the light, scale-1 captures at 390 and 1280 px. One
  re-look covers only the fixed directions (`--only`). In a quick round the
  eye runs only when the text report flags something. The script's text
  report comes first and is free. A heavier option was left aside: an eye
  on every scheme and scale, at every fix.
- **The capture script lives in the skill.** It is Playwright under Deno.
  A screenshot command declared in `.agents/project.md` replaces it for
  what it covers. The skill cites no section number and names no Harness
  tool. Reusing `visual-proof`'s Node script was left aside: it compares a
  before and an after, while `lead-ux` captures each direction, the
  keyboard and an overflow report.
- **No comparison of the delivered screen with the chosen direction at
  the close.** That waits for the visual proof of relecture-humaine (Q6).
  After three rounds without a choice, the Architect asks whether to go on
  (Q7).
- **Review fixes.** The reviewers found one real defect: the overflow
  measure missed a block clipped inside its box at 1280 px. The eye saw it
  and the report did not. The script now also names a box that clips its
  content, and a descendant that passes its direction's box.

## Data schema, before → after

No schema change.

## Architecture change

The socle gains a skill that ships its own script: `lead-ux`, six files
under `socle/agents/skills/lead-ux/`, listed in `src/socle-files.ts` and the
golden tree. The workflow gains one stop, "After each round of `lead-ux`",
and the ordering rule for the push. Nothing else moves.
